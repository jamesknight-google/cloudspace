- **4.1 Availability Guarantee:** Vendor guarantees a monthly Service Availability SLA of **99.95%** across all production endpoints.
- **4.2 Service Credits:** If availability falls below 99.95% in any calendar month, Customer receives:
  - 99.80% - 99.94%: 5% monthly fee credit
  - 99.50% - 99.79%: 15% monthly fee credit
  - Below 99.50%: 30% monthly fee credit.

#### 5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
- **5.1 Standard Aggregate Cap:** Except as provided in Section 5.2, each party’s maximum aggregate liability shall be limited to **one times (1.0x) the Annual Contract Value (AUD $310,000)**.

- **5.3 IP Infringement:** Vendor’s indemnity obligations for intellectual property infringement shall remain uncapped.
