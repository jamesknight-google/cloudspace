### OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

#### 1. SERVICES & PRICING
- **1.1 Scope:** Vendor shall provide real-time APAC FX and ASX/NZX Equities Level 3 order book feeds via low-latency FIX protocol and WebSockets.
- **1.2 Annual Contract Value (ACV):** AUD $420,000 per annum, invoiced quarterly in arrears.

#### 2. DATA HOSTING & SOVEREIGNTY
- **2.1 Primary Infrastructure:** Vendor warrants that all production servers, database nodes, client telemetry, and market data caches are hosted exclusively within the Commonwealth of Australia in AWS Sydney (ap-southeast-2) and AWS Melbourne (ap-southeast-4).
- **2.2 Data Residency:** Under no circumstances shall ANZ transactional data, trading logs, or queries be routed or stored outside the borders of Australia without prior written consent from ANZ Chief Information Security Officer.

#### 3. INFORMATION SECURITY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE (APRA CPS 234 / CPS 230)
- **3.1 Certifications:** Vendor maintains active ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II certifications, audited annually by an independent Big Four accounting firm.
- **3.2 Incident Notification:** Vendor shall notify ANZ Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT) in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of any confirmed or suspected security incident or data breach.
- **3.3 Regulatory & Customer Audit Rights:** Upon reasonable notice, Vendor shall permit ANZ internal auditors, external designees, and APRA regulators to inspect Vendor’s security controls, hosting environments, and disaster recovery procedures.

#### 4. SERVICE AVAILABILITY (SLA) & CREDITS
- **4.1 Monthly Uptime Commitment:** Vendor guarantees a monthly Service Availability of not less than **99.99%** (twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week).
- **4.2 Service Credits:** If monthly availability falls below 99.99%, Customer is entitled to the following fee credits applied to the subsequent invoice:
  - 99.90% to 99.98%: 10% monthly fee credit
  - 99.50% to 99.89%: 25% monthly fee credit
  - Below 99.50%: 50% monthly fee credit + right to terminate for material breach.

#### 5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & INDEMNITIES
- **5.1 Aggregate Cap:** Subject to Section 5.2, each party’s maximum aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be capped at **two times (2.0x) the Annual Contract Value (AUD $840,000)**.
- **5.2 Unlimited Liability Carve-outs:** The liability limitation in Section 5.1 shall not apply to:
  - (a) Vendor's indemnification obligations for intellectual property infringement;
  - (b) Breach of confidentiality obligations (Section 8);
  - (c) Breach of data protection and privacy obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth); or
  - (d) Gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud.

#### 6. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
- **6.1 Governing Law:** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **State of New South Wales, Australia**.
- **6.2 Jurisdiction:** The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of New South Wales and courts entitled to hear appeals therefrom.
