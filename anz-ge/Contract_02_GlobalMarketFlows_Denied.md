B. Customer desires to connect its proprietary trading systems to Vendor’s global cloud infrastructure.

### OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

#### 1. PLATFORM LICENSE & FEES
- **1.1 License:** Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access Vendor’s US and European cloud execution nodes.
- **1.2 Annual Fee:** USD $350,000 (approx. AUD $540,000) payable annually in advance.

#### 2. DATA LOCATION & HOSTING
- **2.1 Global Infrastructure:** Customer acknowledges and agrees that all order logs, pricing telemetry, counterparty identifiers, and algorithmic execution data are hosted and processed across Vendor’s centralized data centers in **Northern Virginia (USA) and Frankfurt (Germany)**.
- **2.2 No Local Hosting Guarantee:** Vendor does not maintain local servers or storage within Australia or New Zealand and does not offer region-locked data residency.

#### 3. SECURITY, AUDIT & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
- **3.1 Security Standard:** Vendor adheres to internal commercial best practices. Vendor conducts annual self-assessments.
- **3.2 Incident Notification:** In the event of a security incident affecting Customer data, Vendor shall notify Customer within **seven (7) business days** of completing its internal post-mortem investigation.
- **3.3 No Audit Rights:** Due to multi-tenant security architecture, Vendor **strictly prohibits** physical or logical inspection, on-site audits, or third-party regulator inspections (including APRA or ASIC) of its systems, source code, or infrastructure.

#### 4. AVAILABILITY (SLA) & DISCLAIMERS
- **4.1 Service Availability:** Vendor targets a system availability of **99.0%** during US core market trading hours.
- **4.2 No Credits or Remedies:** Availability targets are indicative only. Vendor provides the platform on an **"AS-IS" and "AS-AVAILABLE"** basis. Under no circumstances shall Vendor be liable for outage-related financial losses, nor shall Vendor issue fee credits, rebates, or refunds for downtime.

#### 5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
- **5.1 Total Maximum Liability:** To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Vendor’s cumulative aggregate liability for any and all claims, including breach of contract, negligence, data loss, or cyber security breaches, shall be strictly capped at the **total fees paid by Customer in the preceding three (3) months (capped at approximately USD $87,500 / AUD $135,000)**.
- **5.2 Exclusion of Consequential Damages:** Vendor shall have no liability whatsoever for loss of trading profits, regulatory fines, or third-party claims.

#### 6. GOVERNING LAW & ARBITRATION
- **6.1 Governing Law:** This Agreement and any disputes arising out of or relating hereto shall be governed by and construed solely in accordance with the laws of the **State of Delaware, United States of America**, without regard to conflict of law principles.
- **6.2 Binding Arbitration:** All disputes shall be resolved by confidential, binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) in **Wilmington, Delaware, USA**.
